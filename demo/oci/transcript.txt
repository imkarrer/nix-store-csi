== Side by side ==
                       | storePaths                                              | closure (OCI)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pinning                | an immutable store path in the pod spec                 | an immutable digest in the pod spec
Architectures          | one storePaths.<system> list per system                 | one index digest for every system
Promotion, air gap     | nix copy into a reachable cache; nodes need that cache  | crane copy A to B; same digest; no cache on the nodes
Policy                 | admitted; fails later as a mount error                  | refused at admission by registry and digest
Bumps                  | custom CI that evaluates Nix and envsubsts paths        | Renovate (docker datasource) bumps the digest from the tag
Inspection, signing    | nix path-info against a cache; narinfo signatures only  | crane, oras, cosign on the digest; narinfo signatures still checked
exit=0
